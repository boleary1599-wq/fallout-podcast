🎃 F.A.L.L.O.U.T. PODCAST — EPISODE 186: HALLOWEEN SPECIAL! 🎃

THE OTHERWISE — PERFORMED IN FULL FOR THE FIRST TIME EVER!!

This Halloween, F.A.L.L.O.U.T. proudly presents the first-ever complete dramatic performance of The Otherwise, the extraordinary, bizarre and frequently disturbing horror screenplay co-written in 2015 by Mark E. Smith and Graham Duff.

Subtitled The Screenplay for a Horror Film That Never Was, this long-lost project follows members of The Fall as they head to a recording studio near Pendle Hill to make an EP, only to find themselves entangled with satanic biker gangs, time-travelling Jacobites and all manner of supernatural unpleasantness.

Drawing on the weird fiction of Arthur Machen, M. R. James, Philip K. Dick and H. P. Lovecraft, alongside northern folklore, working-class occultism and Smith's own peculiar worldview, it's a gloriously unhinged collision of folk horror, science fiction and Fall mythology.

Originally rejected by film production companies, the screenplay eventually found its way into print courtesy of Strange Attractor Press. But now, for the first time ever, the entire thing is brought to life, from beginning to end, by the F.A.L.L.O.U.T. players!

Joining the usual gang of B, DB and Al are our extraordinary guest performers Emma, MES (not that one!), Harry, Steve Titley and Froze! An ensemble of questionable theatrical pedigree, united in the noble pursuit of bringing this magnificent oddity to life.

Expect satanic bikers, strange encounters, sinister goings-on, grotesque humour and the unmistakable imagination of MES and Graham Duff.
