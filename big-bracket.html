THE BIG BRACKET

THE SURVIVORS.

A clear, round-by-round record of the songs still standing. Winners only. No maze of lines, no cluttered match boxes.

The tournament begins in four eras, merges into two pools in Round 2, then becomes one competition in Round 3. This page shows the names supported by the CORE DOC; incomplete lists are marked clearly rather than guessed.

Source: F.A.L.L.O.U.T. CORE DOC. Round One winner names are extracted from its winners tab; Round Two survivors from the Round Three entrant list; Round Four entrants and Round Five survivors from the later-round notes. Some source lists are partial or inconsistently formatted. No missing names or results have been invented.
