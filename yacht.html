Not a charity. Not an emergency. Not a worthy cause. Just a frankly outrageous ambition to own a Hollywood-style motor yacht.

CAMPAIGN NOT YET LIVE. This is a planning page. No donations are being collected here, and no GoFundMe account or fundraising link has been created.
The proposed target
£1,000,000

Think James Bond, not a rowing boat. The working plan is a second-hand, roughly 60-foot Sunseeker Predator or comparable motor yacht: glamorous enough to be ridiculous, but not a billionaire's superyacht.

Illustrative figures only. Actual vessel prices, taxes, mooring, insurance, repairs, fuel, crew requirements and transaction costs must be verified before a purchase.

One yacht. Two glorious summers. Possibly a resale.

The ambition is to acquire the yacht, use it for a year or two, document the absurdity, and potentially sell it afterwards. It would be a personal luxury purchase, not a charitable project or an investment opportunity.

Supporters would receive no ownership interest, guaranteed return, repayment, financial stake or claim on the vessel or any eventual sale proceeds. Any later sale proceeds would remain with the owner unless a different arrangement were explicitly established and disclosed before donations were accepted.

If the target is not reached, any proposed fundraiser must clearly state how contributions would be used and whether the yacht purchase could proceed. The details of that policy have not yet been decided.

Why would anyone contribute?

Because the idea is funny. Because a podcast that spends its time arguing about The Fall has somehow arrived at the conclusion that its host needs a luxury motor yacht. Because, in a world of worthy fundraising appeals, this one is proudly, spectacularly unworthy.

But the joke must be transparent: this is for a personal luxury purchase, not for charity, a community project or an emergency. No yacht has been purchased or reserved.

What's next?

Confirm eligibility for a supported-country GoFundMe account, decide who legally receives and owns the funds and vessel, verify a real yacht budget, and publish clear policies for an unsuccessful campaign and possible resale.
